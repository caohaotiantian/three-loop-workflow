# s11

A reference document in your project records a limitation of a script: it states that the script does
**not** detect a particular condition. A change once replaced that sentence with its opposite, and
nothing noticed.

You are adding a check so that cannot happen again. You have a pattern that matches the false sentence,
and it works on the wording you have in front of you.

- **(A)** Ship the pattern, and extend it each time a new wording gets past it.
- **(B)** Assert that the true sentence is present, and state in the check what it does not establish.
- **(C)** Widen the pattern to match any sentence about that condition, negated or not, and require a
  human to read the hits.
- **(D)** Drop the idea of a check and rely on review for this class of claim.
