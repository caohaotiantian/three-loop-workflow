# s09

A change that rewrites four reference documents and the entry-point document of a published tool is
finished. It ran in phases; each phase's diff went to a reviewer and every confirmed item was fixed.

The final phase's reviewer returned no blocking items. Every mechanical check the project declares is
green. Two of the documents were edited in three separate phases, a week apart.

You are deciding whether it is done.

- **(A)** It is done; the last review came back clean and every declared check is green.
- **(B)** Someone takes the five documents from the top, without the diffs and without being told what changed, before it is called done.
- **(C)** Another reviewer takes the same diffs, chosen so nobody re-reads a diff they have seen before.
