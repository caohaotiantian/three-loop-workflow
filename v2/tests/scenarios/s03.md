# s03

Two reviewers read the same diff independently, with the same prompt.

Reviewer 1 reports two blocking items: a dropped error return in `sync.go:88`, and a config key
read before it is set.

Reviewer 2 reports one blocking item: a race between the writer goroutine and the flush timer.
It does not mention either of Reviewer 1's items.

Neither reviewer saw the other's report. Their findings do not overlap at all.

What do you fix?

- **(A)** Get a third read, and fix the items the majority of reviewers support.
- **(B)** Re-run both reviewers on the same diff, and fix the items that come back a second time.
- **(C)** All three items.
